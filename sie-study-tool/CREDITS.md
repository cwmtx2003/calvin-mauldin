## FINRA SIE Content Outline

The **FINRA Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) Content Outline** (© FINRA) is
used as the *curriculum outline* that organizes this study aid into sections,
topics, and units. It is not reproduced as study content beyond the outline
structure and the rule references it enumerates. FINRA is the source of truth for
the exam; see <https://www.finra.org>.

## SRO rulebooks — offline grounding only (not redistributed)

The **FINRA**, **MSRB**, and **CBOE** rulebooks were read **offline** during
content generation to verify the accuracy of rules, thresholds, and citations.
Their text is **not** reproduced, embedded, or redistributed in this project — no
SRO rule text ships in the browser. Generated questions cite SRO rule *numbers*
(from the public FINRA outline) so learners can look them up at the source.

## What is generated vs. sourced

- **Generated** (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): flashcards, study guides, reference sheets, the
  practice-question bank, and AI-written explanations.
- **Sourced verbatim, shippable**: the SEC investor.gov glossary definitions
  (public domain) and the FINRA SIE outline text (used as the outline).
- **Sourced, offline only**: SRO rulebooks and full OpenStax texts (excerpts and
  derived study content only; full texts are not committed — see `.gitignore`).
